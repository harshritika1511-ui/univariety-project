Alumni influence — top recruiting targets

Upload an alumni roster (needs at minimum a company and a current-designation column). We rank companies by Alumni Influence Score — the sum of a seniority weight per alumnus at that company — so a company with a few founders and one with many mid-level engineers can both surface, for different reasons. You can switch to ranking by raw headcount or average seniority instead. See "How this is calculated" below for the full method and other mechanisms considered.
